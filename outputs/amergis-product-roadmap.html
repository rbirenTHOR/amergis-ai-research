Product strategy · Phase 4 · 2026-10-20

What to build, what to establish

Build one AI-operated staffing platform with three role-based experiences. Own workflow decisions, policy versions and audit evidence; establish specialist services and choose one authoritative staffing record system.

Structured roadmapAPI evidenceOperating blueprint
ArchitectureProduct portfolioReconstruction feasibilityBuild sequenceNext discovery

One platform, three experiences

Start with one staffing service line, one specialty, one jurisdiction and one client/worksite group. This is a scope recommendation, not a claim the market or client has been selected.

Experiences
  • Worker portal and public jobs
  • Client portal
  • Operations and exception console

Use one application with role-specific access initially; a native mobile app is optional later.

Owned operating layer
  • Durable workflow states
  • Versioned contract and eligibility rules
  • Agent action gateway
  • Approvals, audit and reconciliation

Agents propose or execute permitted actions through validated domain commands. The model is not the authority for payroll totals or credentials.

Records and adapters
  • Selected ATS/CRM or minimal owned staffing core
  • Credential evidence store
  • Time and billing preparation
  • Vendor adapters and event ledger

Assign one authoritative system per record class. Vendor IDs map to internal IDs; duplicate masters are not an acceptable integration design.

Established services
  • Identity and permissions
  • Communications and e-signature
  • Screening and licensing
  • Payroll, accounting and payments
  • Storage, monitoring and backups

Provision the new operator's accounts and permitted integration scope. Amergis's accounts and datasets are not available inputs.

Operating scope

AI can operate delegated administrative tasks. Licensed healthcare and education services, accountable employment/clinical decisions, contractual exceptions and financial release still need the relevant professionals and owners. The current research does not establish a zero-human company.

ATS / CRM selection gate

Establish a suitable ATS/CRM for the initial pilot if it passes the integration criteria. Do not select solely because Amergis historically named a vendor.

  • Current documented read/write APIs for required records and status transitions
  • Permitted AI integration and customer data access under the applicable agreement
  • Scoped service accounts, field access and tenant isolation
  • Synthetic test environment or an acceptable documented test approach
  • Event/change support or reliable incremental reconciliation
  • Data export, attachments, identifiers, limits and operational support
  • Measured fit to the pilot journey and commercial terms

If no candidate passes, build the minimal candidate–job–submission–assignment core for the pilot. Do not recreate an enterprise ATS feature catalog in parallel with buying one.

Erecruit, Bullhorn, ADP, Certiphi, Nursys and MatterWorx are research leads or examples, not selected suppliers. No quotes, contracts or accounts have been obtained.

Release sequence with explicit exit conditions

Sequence is a recommendation; dates, staffing and costs have not been estimated. Each release requires evidence before expanding agent authority.

R0

Operating contract and vendor decisions

Build

Fix the pilot scope; define object ownership, allowed agent actions and one client requirement/rate pack.

Establish

Select ATS approach, payroll/accounting, screening/licensing and communication services; obtain permitted test access.

Exit evidence

An explicit owner and authoritative system for each object; vendor integration gaps recorded; synthetic journey agreed.

R1

Candidate to approved placement

Build

Worker intake and consent, client requisition intake, evidence-linked matching, credential/onboarding queue and operations approvals.

Establish

A consented candidate population and real client demand; test credentials and screening packages for the chosen profession.

Exit evidence

Synthetic duplicate, missing credential, rejection, withdrawal and approved placement cases behave correctly. No live employment decision delegated solely to a model.

R2

Assignment to reconciled money

Build

Shift confirmation, time intake/amendments, pay preparation, invoice draft and reconciliation views.

Establish

Contract-designated time source, payroll codes, billing rules, accounting connections and named financial owners.

Exit evidence

No duplicate booking/pay batch/invoice on retries; pay and bill totals reconcile; a client invoice dispute does not automatically suppress earned-pay processing.

R3

Bounded live operation

Build

Enable approved reminders and record updates; raise task authority only after observed pilot performance.

Establish

Named exception coverage, client/worker agreements and live vendor scopes; thresholds set by accountable owners.

Exit evidence

Measured errors, intervention rates and response times meet agreed limits; rollback, replay and escalation are exercised.

R4

Additional products and scale

Build

Multi-client programs, supplier/VMS portal, advanced analytics, native mobile or teletherapy administration only when their business lines are selected.

Establish

Additional contract/jurisdiction rules and relevant licensed services.

Exit evidence

Each expansion has its own verified workflows, unit economics and regression cases; no assumption one client's configuration generalizes.
