What the initial extraction establishes

The public jobs page directly references a job-card JSON file and a separate searchable text index. Both contained 2,755 records/keys, joined by the feed ID. The card feed exposes state, job type, category and setting plus rendered HTML containing title, pay, duration, location and URL. The database preserves the separate feed, position and URL identifiers. Full descriptions were inspected for availability but are not redistributed here.

The healthcare page displayed 2,751 results, while the education page displayed 2,764. Those observations are not reconciled, and the education catalog has not been fully extracted. The healthcare feed includes 1,567 Nursing, 540 Allied and 345 Aide records, plus other categories; 79 records are tagged Field or Corporate. These are listing categories, not workforce counts. Healthcare job board · Public job-card feed · Public job search index · Education job board

The May 2026 pricing workbook provides 148 hourly not-to-exceed rates. Examples are $115/hour for Med/Surg RNs and $125/hour for school speech-language pathologists. Three proposed MSP tiers depend on projected client spending, with potential fees ranging from 3% to 5% across the bands. VMS fees and rebates are labeled as included, not additive. Exact $5M spend is not explicitly assigned a band. Sourcewell contract 061324-AMH · Sourcewell pricing workbook

Operating workflow reconstructed from evidence

Client demand → contract/requisition → sourcing and screening → candidate submission → client decision → credentialing/onboarding → placement and shifts → time capture → approval → payroll and invoicing → reconciliation, retention and exceptions

This is a synthesized functional model, not a claim about exact internal sequencing. The documented per-diem branch can schedule an existing compliant applicant automatically; candidates with outstanding requirements go to a recruiter. MaxView feature guide · Per-diem workflow guide · National Recruitment Manager historical posting · Time and Expense historical posting

The Knox County packet is especially informative: it describes screening, training, file audits and invoice review, while its customer addendum prefers email and the customer's own timekeeping over a VMS. Consequently, the product design needs client-specific workflow configuration. This is a historical client example, not a universal Amergis procedure. Knox County contract packet

What “AI operated” needs to mean at this stage

The working scope is AI-run staffing administration with human clinicians and educators delivering services. Candidate/job normalization, intake, scheduling assistance, document completeness and invoice reconciliation are promising test areas. The public evidence also includes clinical interviews, incident investigation, client approvals and exceptions, so fully autonomous operation remains a hypothesis to evaluate task by task. Accountable ownership of clinical, employment and financial decisions belongs in the design. Clinical education role · Clinical Investigator

Next extraction priorities

  1. Reconcile job catalogs. Extract education feed if publicly linked; deduplicate by preserved IDs and canonical URLs; separate internal and deployed roles. Daily snapshots only if later requested; current run is one-time.
  2. Build contract rule matrix. Compare multiple recent public agreements for approval routes, rates, overtime, cancellation, credential requirements and invoice terms. Record client, jurisdiction and effective dates for each rule.
  3. Map workforce tasks. Expand internal role catalog and map each task to inputs, outputs, decision rules, exception owner and KPI. Do not infer staffing levels from hiring advertisements.
  4. Resolve current product state. Cross-reference current guides, release notes and publicly available screenshots for MaxView and Connect. Authorized demos or supplied materials needed for nonpublic detail.
  5. Define automation tests. Test job normalization, eligibility checks, document completeness, scheduling constraints and invoice reconciliation on synthetic cases. Clinical and employment decisions retain accountable owners until separately justified.

Coverage and limitations

  • This is a public-source research database, not a copy of Amergis production systems.
  • No access to candidate pool, customer CRM, credential documents, payroll, billing transactions, internal schemas or matching algorithms.
  • Healthcare feed records, healthcare page totals and education page totals are separate observations; not unique vacancy, employee or placement counts.
  • Public descriptions can be historical or aspirational. Documented client procedures can differ from advertised platform workflows.
  • Public bill-rate ceilings and fee proposals do not establish realized prices, costs, profit margins or client revenue.
  • Terms-of-use retrieval returned timeouts; no conclusion about bulk reuse permissions. Robots and rate directives require checking before a larger crawl.
  • No logins, account creation, form submissions, security testing, private endpoint enumeration or scheduled monitoring were performed.
