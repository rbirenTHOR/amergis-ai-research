planned absence
Confirm availability, district acceptance and exact scope; schedule paid handoff/preparation where required. Backup gains only necessary assignment access. Do not silently treat them as the original provider.

Agent alerts operations and drafts impacted-session list; human contacts the district through agreed channels. Offer the already cleared backup only if actually available and approved for the assignment.
no available backup
Report unfilled work honestly and preserve missed-service evidence. The district/IEP process decides educational remedy; no automated makeup or compensatory-service determination.
commercial rule
Choose and price a real availability arrangement: paid scheduled reserve, limited standby retainer or explicitly non-guaranteed on-call. Employment counsel/payroll determine compensable on-call restrictions. Do not promise guaranteed coverage for an unpaid name on a roster.
access rule
Log handoff, new clinician attestation and any change of record access; revoke access when coverage ends.

calendar note
Do not multiply a normalized service month by 12 to forecast a school-year contract. Actual start date, school calendar, closures, assignment changes and June 30, 2027 term end must replace this normalized view.
invoice note
The RFP requires invoice and attendance records within 30 calendar days of service. That is a submission deadline, not payment Net 30. Fee-for-service narrative is requested; no fixed price/unit worksheet is provided.
starting position
User-confirmed new company, no confirmed therapists and no insurance in place. This model budgets setup rather than assuming an existing operating platform, approved roster or bound coverage.
  • Contribution before shared overhead is not operating profit. Operating profit charges the owner/operator’s human work, insurance and tools; startup costs and financing still need recovery.
  • Cash funding is a conservative no-collection reserve policy, not a predicted cash-flow low point. It funds setup, annual prepaid premiums and all operating outflows to modeled first cash plus a 30-day cushion, then adds 10%. It does not net receivables.
  • Annual insurance is expensed monthly for economics, prepaid in cash by default, and excluded from monthly cash burn to avoid counting the same premium twice. Change cash treatment if the broker offers installments, then include financing charges.
  • A backup allowance buys neither a cleared named therapist nor a contractual response time. Actual cover has to be agreed. Unused allowance is reserve, not proof of delivered service.
  • The owner can defer cash pay only as an explicit financing choice. The economic cost remains in operating profit.
  • A short or fragmented assignment can be much less economic than this normalized month; quote only after the site schedule, allowed units, meetings, no-shows, cancellations and travel terms are settled.
  • Do not raise clinical session intensity to hit an economic utilization target. Clinical workload and IEP obligations remain controlled by qualified staff and district approvals.
  • All cash targets are total funding required for a fresh pilot at that scale; incremental funding is the difference from the prior scale. It is not a per-clinician quote. Scale economies assume the same shared platform and cost structure and must be revalidated before adding clinicians.
  • The maximum affordable pay calculation diagnoses a rate/schedule mismatch. It is not a recommended offer or permission to reduce legally owed pay. Renegotiate authorized pricing/scope or decline uneconomic work.
