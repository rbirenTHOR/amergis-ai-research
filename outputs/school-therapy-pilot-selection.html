Recommendation

East Ramapo has a current SLP-specific purchasing route and explicitly contemplates part-time individual sessions through full-time leave coverage. Rockland BOCES has a second nearby discipline-specific procurement. This combination provides the strongest verified first-pilot prospect in the markets screened, provided the entity and named clinicians can meet the requirements.

Target selection, not bid readiness.

This is a recommendation about where to validate and pursue a pilot, not a finding that the new company is bid-ready, that these buyers will award work, or that New York has the best margins.

The two New York bids close October 29–30, 2026. Recheck addenda and readiness before deciding whether to respond. If the team is not ready, retain the region as a research target and seek the next permitted entry window; do not promise capacity to meet a deadline.

Proposed first assignment

One buyer, one discipline, initially one experienced clinician and a cleared backup; expand toward three only against approved assignments. In-person delivery for East Ramapo. Pilot size is our proposal, not a buyer commitment.

Ongoing district route

Frederick County Public Schools, Maryland has a verified route to add qualified providers during the contract term. Its experience and reference provisions make it a conditional alternative, especially for an established operator.

Different service model

NYCPS independent-provider registration has no fixed deadline identified, but it serves a family-held RSA voucher channel. That is a different acquisition and service model from district staffing.
