Scope & findings

Selected market

Schools and therapists

Worked example

Speech-language pathology staffing for a school district; OT and PT variants must be configured separately

Open jurisdiction

United States baseline research; state not selected

Delivery and data

Staffing-first example. District-approved modality; in-person, virtual and hybrid eligibility are not assumed interchangeable.

Administration can be delegated. Clinical and educational authority stays explicit.

Qualified professionals and the authorized school team own evaluation, eligibility, service plans, therapy delivery, clinical attestations and decisions about missed services. Agents support administration.

Keep student clinical and IEP content in the district-designated system for the initial design. Use permitted opaque references and minimum approved aggregates only; an opaque reference can still be protected education data.

What changed from the broad staffing roadmap

CapabilitySchool-therapy requirement

New evidence that changes the design

  • Track clinician work time, student service delivery, and customer billable units separately.
  • The district may require its own timekeeping, student-record and teletherapy systems. A new vendor portal is not necessarily the system of record.
  • Provider credentials, assistant eligibility, delivery modality, calendar, billing and missed-service handling require district-specific rule versions.
  • AI can prepare, reconcile and flag administrative work. The district and qualified professionals retain service eligibility, IEP, assessment, treatment and clinical signoff decisions.
  • Public solicitations reveal useful requirements but do not prove an award, current Amergis execution, customer demand available to us or permission to access student records.

                  rules

                  tenant and storage controls
                  • Tenant row scopes and foreign-key checks apply to database, queues, object storage, search/vector index, caches, exports and logs.
                  • Client/worksite membership adds a second boundary inside tenant; workers see own data, not tenant-wide pool.

                  • Provider account/connection is tenant-bound; webhook signatures and provider account mapping validated before use.
                  • Cross-tenant or cross-client record discovery returns no existence/detail leak; authorization evaluated before search and retrieval.

                  • Student clinical/service records are out of scope. Incident intake minimizes identifiers and routes necessary care details to authorized clinical channels.
