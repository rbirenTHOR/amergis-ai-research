These are proposed integration boundaries. They do not expand the task authority defined in the previous blueprint or establish live automation.

Recommended architecture

Agents request domain actions through a policy-controlled service. Vendor adapters handle credentials, validation, idempotency and event acknowledgement. Separate preparation, approval and execution; keep the event ledger and exception queue outside any one vendor.
